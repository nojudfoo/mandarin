Pronunciation Guide
The Four Tones
Every syllable in Mandarin has one of four tones — the same syllable means completely different things depending on the tone.
1st tone — ā (flat, high)
Hold it high and steady, like singing a note. mā = mother
2nd tone — á (rising)
Rise like asking a question. má = hemp / numb
3rd tone — ǎ (dip)
Dip down then back up, like saying "huh?" mǎ = horse
4th tone — à (falling)
Drop sharply, like giving an order. mà = to scold
Neutral tone — a (unstressed)
Short and light, no tone mark. ma = question particle 吗
